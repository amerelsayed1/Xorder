## Summary
A **shift** is one cashier session on one till (terminal): opened with an opening float, taking sales and cash movements, closed with a counted drawer. The till writes shifts to its own branch PostgreSQL first and sends them to the cloud afterwards (contract POS-BE-009, status READY: `apps/xorder-cloud/docs/coordination/BACKEND_TO_POS.md:203`). The cloud stores them, computes its own expected cash and variance, and the dashboard home shows one "current shift" panel.

## Scope and exclusions
Implemented:
- Open, current, close (cash-up), X/Z report, cash in/out on the cloud API (`C:routes/api.php:814-818`).
- POS: open, current, summarise, close with approval for another cashier's drawer, branch-wide activity and history (`P:lib/domain/repositories/repositories.dart:851-900`, `P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_shift_repository.dart`), cash movements (`P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_cash_movements_repository.dart`).
- POS→cloud delivery through the sync outbox (`P:lib/data/cloud/outbox_drain.dart`).
- Dashboard "current shift" tiles (`W:src/pages/dashboard/HomeView.vue:179-190`).

Not found in code (searches recorded in `docs/FEATURE_INVENTORY.md` "Absent" and the validation report): no dashboard or admin endpoint to list, view or edit shifts, no Vue shift-history page, and no client of `GET /api/pos/shifts/current` or `GET /api/pos/shifts/{shift}/report` anywhere in the POS, Vue or packages sources (the POS reads its own database).

## Current implemented behavior
**Cloud**
1. Open: replay-safe by `client_transaction_id` (same hash → 200 with the existing shift, different payload → 422 `IDEMPOTENCY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`); the branch must exist in the tenant (404 `BRANCH_NOT_IN_TENANT`) but is **not** required to be the terminal's own branch; one open shift per terminal, enforced under a row lock on the terminal (422 `SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN`); client `opened_at` is normalised to the app time zone (same instant) (`C:app/Services/PosShiftService.php:35-91`).
2. Current: latest open shift of the requesting terminal or `null` (`PosShiftService.php:147-153`).
3. Close: resolved by server id or client uuid within the terminal's **branch**; a closed shift with the same close hash is a replay (this check runs before the capability check); a different close payload → 422 `SHIFT_NOT_OPEN`; the server computes expected cash and variance; a non-zero variance requires `variance_reason` (422 `VALIDATION_FAILED`); writes audit row `pos_shift.closed` (`PosShiftService.php:94-145`).
4. Cash movement: needs an open shift of the same terminal (422 `SHIFT_NOT_OPEN`), idempotent on `client_transaction_id` (`PosShiftService.php:156-202`).
5. Report (X/Z): totals by tender, orders count, cash in/out, refunds, expected cash; scoped to the requesting terminal's branch (`PosShiftService.php:210-245`).
6. Shift attribution on the cloud: when an order is created, and when a payment is added to an existing order, the request's own `shift_client_id` is resolved to an **open** shift of the same terminal (`C:app/Services/PosOrderService.php:96,321,503-513`). An order whose shift does not resolve is stored unattributed. A payment whose shift does not resolve gets the order's `pos_shift_id` (`PosOrderService.php:444`). Refunds resolve their shift without an open-status filter (`C:app/Services/PosRefundService.php:330-340`).
7. Dashboard tile: the tenant-wide latest open shift by `opened_at` (one shift even when several tills are open), with sales = all payments of that shift and expected cash from `PosShiftService::expectedCash` (`C:app/Http/Controllers/Dashboard/StatsController.php:132-157`).

**POS**
0. Sales, refunds and audit rows are stamped with the open shift through `openShiftIdFor` (`P:lib/data/postgres/open_shift.dart:23`; callers in `postgres_orders_repository.dart`, `postgres_refunds_repository.dart`, `postgres_admin_repositories.dart`).
1. Open returns the already-open shift of this terminal instead of failing (a restarted till continues), otherwise inserts into `pos_shifts` and, in modes that sync sales to the platform, queues a `shift_open` outbox event in the same transaction (`P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_shift_repository.dart:66-119`).
2. Close computes the summary and variance inside the same transaction as the update, stores them, refuses a second close (`WHERE closed_at IS NULL`, then a `StateError`), and queues `shift_close` (`postgres_shift_repository.dart:343-430`). The permission and approval check is called before the transaction (`:350-360`); the variance is computed inside it (`:366-372`).
3. Cash movements: a `drawer_open` is stored as a `pos_cash_movements` row (amount 0, counted in `drawerOpens`) but gets no client id and no outbox row; the other kinds get a client uuid and an outbox row in the same transaction, and only when the mode syncs sales to the platform (`P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_cash_movements_repository.dart:162-200`). Supplier pay-outs from the drawer (and their reversals, which write a `pay_in` while the shift is open) also create `cash_movement` outbox rows only in sync mode (`P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_supplier_invoices_repository.dart:826-828,848,1009-1034,1125-1140`).
4. The drain runs its phases in this order: customers, shift opens, orders, cash movements and refunds, shift closes (`P:lib/data/cloud/outbox_drain.dart:280-296`). Each phase reads at most 25 rows ordered by `enqueued_at` (`passLimit` at `:74`; queries at `:309-321` and `:459-468`). What that does and does not guarantee: `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` A.
5. POS cash policy: a pay-out above the configured ceiling `cash.pay_out_max_minor` is refused in the cash dialog without an approval prompt (`P:lib/features/cash/presentation/cash_dialogs.dart:286-297`, `P:lib/domain/models/pos_settings.dart:80,240`), and a per-person limit key `cash.pay_out.max` exists (`P:lib/domain/models/permissions.dart:357-359`); with `cash.blind_close` on, the expected figure and variance are hidden while counting unless the closer holds `shift.view_expected_cash` (`P:lib/features/shift/presentation/shift_dialogs.dart:274-276,501-503`, `P:lib/domain/models/pos_settings.dart:78,219-240`). Independently of the policy, the Z report shown after the close is masked (tenders, cash in/out, expected cash, variance) for anyone lacking `cash.variance.view` (`shift_dialogs.dart:591-598`, `ZReportView` from `:691`); the closed dialog's subtitle, however, always reads "balances" or "recorded with a variance of X" (`shift_dialogs.dart:576-579`, rendered at `:984`), whatever the key. Tests: `P:test/features/close_shift_blind_test.dart` covers the pre-count view only (non-holders under a blind policy, `shift.view_expected_cash` holders at `:164-186`, the pre-count Z report panel); no test covers the post-close mask (no test references `ZReportView` or the closed-shift title); the over/short mask is tested only for the Finance › Settlements pane (`P:test/features/shift_history_panes_test.dart:411,475-498`, a sibling pane on the same history frame); no test asserts the People › Shifts pane's masked over/short column or its blind closed-row report (`management_shifts_pane.dart:101-115`). No caller in `lib` or `bin` writes the `cash.blind_close` key (only its constant and the policy reader; a generic settings repository `save` exists and a test writes the key through it, `P:test/data/wave_b_repositories_test.dart:179-195`), so how it is set is unknown (see Unknowns).
6. A close also writes an `AuditAction.shiftClosed` audit row with the counted, expected and variance figures and `approvedBy` (`P:lib/features/shift/presentation/shift_dialogs.dart:413-430`), which the history query reads (`postgres_shift_repository.dart:259-285`). The handover approval (another cashier closing) is recorded only in the POS local audit with action `shift.close.handover` (`shift_dialogs.dart:364`); the approver is not stored on `pos_shifts` and is not sent to the cloud (the close event selects no approver, `outbox_drain.dart:726-770`).

## Edge cases and failure handling
- Till restarted mid-shift: POS returns the open shift (`postgres_shift_repository.dart:70-76`); test `a till restarted mid-service comes back to the same shift` (`P:test/data/postgres_shift_test.dart:178`).
- Two concurrent opens: the cloud open path takes `lockForUpdate` on the terminal row inside `DB::transaction` (`C:app/Services/PosShiftService.php:57-61`) and the POS has a partial unique index (`P:lib/data/postgres/migrations.dart:323-324`); effect under PostgreSQL is not tested. Further concurrency conclusions: `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` I.
- Open and cash-movement replay lookups are tenant-wide, not terminal-scoped (`C:app/Services/PosShiftService.php:39,163`; the tenant scope comes from `BelongsToRestaurant`).
- Opened offline, synced later: client `opened_at` is kept; close can use the client uuid because the shift has no server id yet (`PosShiftService.php:296-300`).
- Drawer short or over: POS records, does not refuse (`P:test/data/postgres_shift_test.dart:334,362`); the cloud requires a reason, so the POS always sends one, generated from its own figures when the cashier gave no note (`P:lib/data/cloud/outbox_drain.dart:694-722`).
- Server rejection classification: the drain **quarantines** the record only for `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `IDEMPOTENCY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`, `MENU_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `BRANCH_NOT_IN_TENANT`, `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND`, `REFUND_EXCEEDS_SOLD`; every other code, including `SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN`, `SHIFT_NOT_OPEN`, `POS_CAPABILITY_MISSING`, `TERMINAL_REVOKED`, `POS_FEATURE_DISABLED` and unknown codes, is **deferred** (`P:lib/data/cloud/outbox_drain.dart:119-130`; the `classify` tests at `P:test/data/outbox_drain_test.dart:245-279` assert `POS_CAPABILITY_MISSING`, `POS_FEATURE_DISABLED`, `SUBSCRIPTION_LAPSED`, `TERMINAL_REVOKED`, unknown and null codes defer and `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `MENU_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `IDEMPOTENCY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH`, `BRANCH_NOT_IN_TENANT`, `REFUND_EXCEEDS_SOLD` quarantine; `SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN`, `SHIFT_NOT_OPEN` and `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` are not asserted by name). Rows that cannot be built are quarantined locally before anything is sent: `LOCAL_SOURCE_MISSING` (an incomplete shift, cash-movement or customer payload, a cash movement of a kind that maps to no direction, a shift close with any null close field, a client-id mismatch on a shift open, shift close, cash movement, customer or refund row, a shift whose own `client_transaction_id` is null, an unknown `event_type`), `SALE_NEVER_QUEUED`, `REFUND_HAS_NO_LINES`, `TENDER_NOT_FROM_PLATFORM` (`P:lib/data/cloud/outbox_drain.dart:90-103,335-338,601-614,637,680,741-749,791-802,854-861`). Refunds can be deferred locally while waiting for their sale (`_Waiting`, `:869-880`).
- Drain ordering, windows, retry timing, multi-shift sequences, exceptions: `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` A, B, C.
- A `drawer_open` is never sent because it gets no client id and no outbox row (`postgres_cash_movements_repository.dart:165-170`).

## Security considerations
- Tenancy: `PosShift` uses `BelongsToRestaurant` (`C:app/Models/PosShift.php:18`) and the device middleware sets the tenant context from the terminal (`C:app/Http/Middleware/PosDeviceAuth.php:77`) and `restaurant_id` comes from the terminal, not the request (`PosShiftService.php:72`). A shift id from another branch resolves to "Unknown shift" (`PosShiftService.php:303-313`).
- Known weakness: if `acting_user_id` is null, no capability is checked and attribution is terminal-only (`PosShiftService.php:320-322`, "v1 relaxation"; the same relaxation exists for orders, `C:tests/Feature/Pos/PosOrdersTest.php:350`).
- `resolve()` filters shifts by the terminal's branch, not by terminal (`PosShiftService.php:301-304`).
- `open` does not require `branch_id` to be the terminal's own branch (`PosShiftService.php:49-53`), while `resolve()` filters by the terminal's branch (`:303`). Consequence: `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` H.
- Replays return before the capability check for all three writes (open `PosShiftService.php:39-47`, close `:99-107`, movement `:163-171`); the capability checks are at `:55`, `:107` and `:182`, after those returns.
- Audit: close writes `pos_shift.closed` with the figures (`PosShiftService.php:131-142`). The `Auditable` trait on `PosShift` (and on `PosCashMovement`, `C:app/Models/PosCashMovement.php:17`) also records `created`, `updated` (with the changed attributes only, skipped when nothing auditable changed) and `deleted` events (`C:app/Models/Concerns/Auditable.php:24-26,32-37`) (`BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` J).
- The POS approval is an in-process object spent once, not a token the cloud can verify (`P:lib/domain/services/authorization.dart:51-60`; the cloud receives no approver).
- Rate limit middleware `throttle:240,1` on the route group (`C:routes/api.php:787`); its bucket key and effect are not tested.

## Known limitations
- v1 relaxation: unattributed (null `acting_user_id`) shifts, movements and orders are accepted.
- No shift list/detail in the dashboard; the home panel shows one tenant-wide latest open shift.
- Handover approval is local only (not on `pos_shifts`, not synced).
- `GET current`/`GET report` have no capability checks and no client; the contract and the build differ (see Cross-application dependencies).
- Cloud and till compute "cash sales" differently (BR-2); `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` G.
- The POS queues `shift_close` only when the mode syncs sales to the platform and the shift has a `client_transaction_id` (`P:lib/data/postgres/postgres_shift_repository.dart:373-376`).
- Refunds attribute to a shift without requiring it to be open (`C:app/Services/PosRefundService.php:330-340`); `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` G.
- `open` branch mismatch (Security) is not guarded.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC-1: A terminal can have at most one open shift; a second open on the same terminal is refused (cloud 422 `SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN`) or returns the existing one (POS).
- AC-2: Repeating an open, close or cash movement with the same `client_transaction_id` and the same hashed fields (BR-9) returns the original result without creating a second row.
- AC-3: Closing stores expected cash, counted cash and variance; expected = float + cash sales + cash in − cash out − cash refunds.
- AC-4: A close with a non-zero variance and no reason is refused by the cloud.
- AC-5: The cloud refuses a cash movement unless the named shift of the terminal is open; on the POS the open-shift requirement is enforced in the cash dialogs and in the supplier pay-out repository, not in `record()` or the schema (BR-8).
- AC-6: Another cashier's drawer closes at the till only with an approval from a different person holding `shift.close`.
- AC-7: The report is limited to the requesting terminal's branch.
- AC-8: A shift opened with a client time and uuid keeps those times and can be closed by that uuid.
- AC-9: Within one drain pass the phases run in the order customers, shift opens, orders, cash movements and refunds, shift closes (`outbox_drain.dart:280-296`); a pass stops after a phase that reports offline (`:295`). Further reading: `BEHAVIORAL_ANALYSIS.md` A.
- AC-10: An order or payment reaching the cloud is counted toward the open shift of the same terminal named by the request's `shift_client_id`; one exception: a payment with no resolvable shift gets its order's shift (BR-12).
- AC-11: Opening a shift with no open business day opens one first, and the shift opens even if the day cannot be opened (`pos-business-day` AC-1, AC-4). Added 2026-10-24; not covered by this folder's TEST_REPORT.
