## Which QR menu / online-ordering products are actually sold in Egypt and MENA, and how do they price and package plans?

### Takeaway
The market splits into three price bands:
1. **Regional ordering platforms** sell annual contracts, typically USD 450–2,300 per year, with branch caps of 2 or 5 per tier. Zyda, Ordable and Mnasati are here, plus Foodics, where online ordering is an add-on inside a POS bundle.
2. **Local Egyptian and Saudi QR-menu startups** run roughly USD 4–40 per month, or USD 35–400 per year. Ordering, WhatsApp and table ordering are the upsell.
3. **Global generic tools**, such as MenuTiger at USD 0/17/46/119 per month, cap the number of orders.

Almost everyone advertises **0% commission** and a flat subscription. The exceptions are Queno (Egypt), which charges a per-order fee only, and Zyda's Egyptian call-centre add-on (3% per order).

### Inferences
- **Three price bands (USD/year):**
  - Pure QR menu, Egypt: about USD 35–60. OrOMenu USD 34, Egypt Menus Pro USD 57, Taaj Digital Menu USD 57.
  - QR plus WhatsApp/table ordering, Egypt and KSA local sellers: about USD 190–370. Taaj Order and Pay USD 191, Egypt Menus Max USD 191, 6lb Professional USD 368, Yalla Pro USD 365. Talb sits at about USD 227/yr at the monthly rate.
  - Regional full-ordering platforms: about USD 430–2,300. Ordable QR-with-ordering USD 428, Ordable Starter USD 534, Zyda Starter USD 782, Mnasati Basic KSA USD 933, Foodics Advanced KSA USD 2,253 per branch, which includes POS.
  - Xorder's feature set (QR, WhatsApp checkout, table ordering, pickup and delivery, multi-branch, reviews, analytics) maps to the top of the second band or the bottom of the third.
- **Flat subscription with 0% commission is the norm.** Zyda, Ordable, Mnasati, MenuTiger, Egypt Menus, Taaj and Talb all advertise no commission. The per-order models are Queno (fee only) and Zyda Ring in Egypt (3%). A commission model would be a differentiator in Egypt only if it removes the upfront fee, as Queno does.
- **Annual billing dominates.** Ordable Egypt and Taaj are annual-only, and Zyda is yearly or quarterly. Annual discounts cluster at about 15–25%: MenuTiger 15%, Ordable "up to 15%", OrOMenu 25%, Menux "2 months free" (about 17%), Egypt Menus about 17%. Monthly plans exist mainly at the cheap end.
- **Most common upgrade triggers:** branch count (Zyda 2→5, Ordable 2→5, Egypt Menus 1→4→10), then item caps (Egypt Menus 150/300/1,000; Mnasati 400/1,500), order caps (MenuTiger 75/500/2,000), staff seats (Mnasati 5/10; Taaj; Menux).
- **Feature upgrade triggers:** the step from menu-only to ordering (6lb, Yalla, Taaj, Ordable QR), and from pickup/delivery to table ordering (6lb Professional, MenuTiger Premium, Yalla Pro).
- **Common add-ons, priced separately:** dine-in/table ordering per branch (Zyda), WhatsApp inbox and campaigns (Zyda), loyalty (Zyda, Ordable Growth, Mnasati Premium), reviews (Zyda), own branded app (Zyda), call centre (Zyda), POS and printer modules (MenuTiger), white-label domain (MenuTiger, Ordable).
- **Free and trial patterns:** a perpetual free menu-only tier (MenuTiger, Menux, Mnasati QR, OrOMenu credits) or a 7–30-day trial (Taaj 7/14, Menux 7, Talb 28, Zyda dine-in 30, Zyda WhatsApp 14). Setup fees are rarely published. Talb's POS install is 1,500 EGP. Zyda says setup is "with Zyda's team".
- **Pricing basis:** per account with branch caps for the ordering platforms (Zyda, Ordable, Egypt Menus); per branch for POS-led Foodics; per business for OrOMenu.
- **Local Egyptian pricing is volatile.** Egypt Menus and OrOMenu prices visible in search snippets were 33–100% lower than the live pages. Any comparison should be re-checked close to launch.
