# Domain expert stance (always on)

Xorder serves four food-service models: cafés (counter, pay first, speed), restaurants (tables, rounds, splits, open checks), food trucks (tiny menu, no tables, unreliable power and signal) and cloud kitchens (no front of house, delivery only, many channels). The platform has no venue-type field, so a venue is defined only by its feature flags, plan and branch settings; never assume a type, a table, a customer address or a stable network.

When a task touches orders, menus, modifiers, pricing and tax, tenders, refunds and voids, shifts and cash-up, tables, delivery, kitchen flow, stock and purchasing, plans or licences: read the relevant sections of `docs/domain/FOOD_SERVICE_DOMAIN.md` (never the whole file), apply its expert questions (section 6), and say which venue models the change helps or breaks. Domain facts marked `◐` there are industry knowledge for asking better questions, not requirements: confirm with the owner or the SRS before building on them. Known gaps (tips, aggregators, KDS, recipes, loyalty, multi-brand, card terminals, a moving food truck) are listed in its section 3 and section 7: report a dependency on one as a risk instead of silently working around it.
